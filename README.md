To get absolutely all worldwide, sports, Bangladeshi, Indian, and Nepali channels in one place with a traditional 1-click P+ / P- remote zapping experience, you won't find a single pre-packaged app that has everything. Because of broadcasting rights, legitimate pre-loaded apps lock down specific regions and rarely support seamless hardware button mapping.

The best solution is to use a dedicated IPTV Player and load it with a custom M3U playlist. This gives you the exact "cable TV" feel on your Smart TV while also working on your mobile device.

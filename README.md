### Installation Guide
Since this application is not available on the Google Play Store, please follow these steps to install the APK on your Android device:
1. Click the banner image above to download the `app-release.apk` file.
2. Once the download is complete, tap on the notification to open the file.
3. If your phone prompts a security warning saying "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source," click on **Settings**.
4. Toggle on the switch that says **Allow from this source** (or "Install Unknown Apps").
5. Go back to the installation screen and tap **Install**.
6. Once the installation is finished, tap **Open** to launch the app.
